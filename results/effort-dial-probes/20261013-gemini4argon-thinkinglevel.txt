Which thinking levels does Gemini 4 Argon accept on the Gemini API, and is "high" its top tier? - 2026-10-13

VERDICT: high IS THE CEILING. The endpoint validates thinkingLevel in two layers and accepts only low, medium
and high for this model. Max does not exist.

POST https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models/gemini-4-argon:generateContent,
generationConfig.thinkingConfig.thinkingLevel = ..., one short counting prompt, n=1 each.

    thinkingLevel   result
    bogus_zzz       400  Invalid value at 'generation_config.thinking_config.thinking_level' (enum), "bogus_zzz"
    minimal         400  Thinking level MINIMAL is not supported for this model.
    low             200  thoughtsTokenCount absent (no thinking reported)
    medium          200  thoughtsTokenCount 79
    high            200  thoughtsTokenCount 408
    max             400  Invalid value ... "max" (not a member of the enum)
    ultra           400  Invalid value ... "ultra"
    field omitted   200  thoughtsTokenCount 79 - the same as medium, so medium is the default

READ THE TWO REJECTIONS SEPARATELY. `max`, `ultra` and the invented value fail SCHEMA validation - they are not
members of the API's ThinkingLevel enum. `minimal` is a member and fails a PER-MODEL check. A bad value cannot be
silently accepted on this route.

MAGNITUDE: one prompt, n=1 - low < medium (79) < high (408). A 5x step from medium to high, but a single sample:
read it as "the dial is read", not as a measured separation.

ON THE BENCH RUN: the harness (Gemini CLI 0.63.0) does not send a thinking level for this model, so the bench
gateway pins thinkingLevel=high on every request and reads it back. The run's receipts record
wire_thinkingLevel={"high": 120} on repo 1 and {"high": 112} on repo 2, and served model gemini-4-argon on every call.
