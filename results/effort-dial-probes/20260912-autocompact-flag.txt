A THIRD RUN, ADDED AFTER THE FACT, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT SAY. The same job was run once more with
no flag at all, as the 200K arm of a separate probe (20260912-compaction-trigger-window.txt). It
compacted zero times and finished at a context of 63,479 tokens - HIGHER than either flagged run's
first trigger (61,981 and 63,093). That is not evidence that the unflagged trigger is higher: the
model simply stopped after three of the six files and answered, so the run ended before it could
cross anything. It is recorded here because a reader comparing the two receipts will notice it, and
because leaving it out would make the flagged pair look cleaner than it is. The verdict above rests
only on A against B, which are the two runs that differ in exactly one thing.

WHY THIS WAS RUN. Two arms of the Sep 12 wave died on terminal_reason=rapid_refill_breaker
after the harness compacted them at ~55K instead of ~290K, because the arms carried no
context declaration. There is no [204k] or [262k] model-string suffix - only [1m] - so this
flag is the only honest way to tell the harness a 204,800 or 262,144-token window. It had
never been tested, and the one field observation available (gpt-oss at --autocompact 100000
triggering at 52,023 and 57,418, against 52,252-76,434 with no flag) does not clear this
board's magnitude rule at n=2. Hence a controlled pair.

METHOD NOTE. The control is the other run, not a remembered baseline, so the result does not
depend on knowing what window the CLI assumes for an unsuffixed model id. Files are 1,900
lines to sit under Claude Code's 2,000-line read truncation, so each read lands whole and
the context grows in known steps. No aggregator is involved, so no arm's credits delta can
absorb this.
